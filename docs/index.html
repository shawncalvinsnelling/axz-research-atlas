Scientific responsibility

What a status label means

No claim without its supporting evidence.

PROVED marks a supplied mathematical argument within its stated scope. FINITE_PROVED means a bounded computation or certificate. CANDIDATE marks a research target. BLOCKED identifies an unsupported or overextended conclusion. A source repository is a starting point for review, not automatically an endorsement.
