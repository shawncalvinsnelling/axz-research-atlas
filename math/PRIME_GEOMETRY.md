# Prime Geometry and Filter-First Protocol

The early prime work produced one important correction:

> Raw prime resonance is not a valid object until guaranteed composite structure is filtered out.

## Proved example

The Ulam corner lane

\[
C_0(r)=(2r+1)^2
\]

is a dead prime lane for \(r\ge 1\), because every term is an odd square.

## Candidate object

For a polynomial lane \(f(r)\), define local obstruction counts

\[
\nu_f(p)=\#\{r\bmod p:f(r)\equiv0\pmod p\}.
\]

Candidate lane ranking should be based on local obstruction products / singular-series factors, not raw finite density.

## Blocked boundary

Finite prime-density scans do not prove:

- infinitely many primes on a lane;
- global RH;
- permanent density plateaus;
- cryptographic security.
