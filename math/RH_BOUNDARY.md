# RH Boundary Statement

This atlas does not claim to prove the Riemann Hypothesis.

The RH-related contribution is a verification posture:

```text
finite zero certificates are useful;
finite scans do not imply global RH;
global RH requires a bridge theorem or standard analytic proof.
```

## Allowed statement

AXZ work may discuss finite-height certificate scaffolds, finite receipts, and defect-boundary verification.

## Blocked statement

AXZ work must not claim:

```text
RH solved
RH validated to infinity
prime resonance proves RH
```
